# Changelog

## 0.1.2

**New**
- **Bell Mob slider:** one slider, "Bell Mob (Deep, Nightmare, Tainted)", for all of the Bell mob's sounds, including its deep bell. Deep, Nightmare and Tainted are the same mob and share these sounds. The Reception Bell stays on its own slider.
- **Charlemange slider:** "Charlemange (Mini Bell)" has its own slider, separate from the Bell mob.

**Changed**
- Charlemange's sounds are no longer on the default list. Search for them, or turn on "Show every sound".

## 0.1.1

**New**
- **NPC Voices slider:** one slider, "NPC Voices (Traders, Villagers)", for all NPC speech. Traders and villagers share overlapping voices, so they can't be told apart.

**Fixed**
- **Recently heard** now lists every sound that played in the last 30 seconds. Before, it also hid sounds that aren't on the default list.

**Known limit**
- NPCs from other mods that use their own voice files are not covered by the NPC Voices slider.

First release.
