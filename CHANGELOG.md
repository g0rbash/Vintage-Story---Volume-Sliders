# Changelog

## 0.1.4

**New**
- **Helve Hammer has its own slider.** The helve hammer sounds nothing like hammering an anvil by hand, but the game gives it no sound file of its own: it plays the hand hammer's hit at a much lower pitch (0.3 to 0.5 instead of about 1), which is also why it resembles the sound of placing an anvil. The mod now tells the two apart by pitch, so the new **Helve Hammer** slider quiets only the helve hammer.

**Changed**
- **Anvil group:** "Helve Hammer" and "Hand Hammer" (the old "Helve Hammer and Anvil" slider: hammering at an anvil by hand and the weld hit) now sit together under an **Anvil** group. Your old setting carries over to the Helve Hammer slider (the one most people used it for), so hand hammering returns to full volume until you turn Hand Hammer down.
- **Charlemange:** the combined Charlemange slider is gone, since the mini Bell sounds nothing like the Bell mob. Its sounds are still there, one slider each, under Mobs > Bell (search for them, or turn on "Show every sound").
- **Bells under Mobs:** the "Bell Mob" slider is now called **Bells** and lives inside the Mobs group, so the Mobs slider scales it too. Your saved Bell Mob setting carries over.

## 0.1.3

**New**
- **Armor sounds:** worn armor now shows on the default list as an **Armor** group with one slider per type: Brigandine, Chain, Leather, Plate and Scale. The Armor slider controls all of them. Before, you had to turn on "Show every sound" to find them.

**Changed**
- **Helve Hammer and Anvil:** the weld hit now shares the Helve Hammer slider, which is now named "Helve Hammer and Anvil". The helve hammer and the player striking an anvil play the same hit sounds, so one slider covers both.
- **Shorter names:** the combined sliders are now just "Bell Mob", "Charlemange" and "NPC Voices", and a few sound names lost their brackets.
- **Miscellaneous:** everyday sounds that aren't what people usually tune, such as blocks, doors, chests, tools, held items, menus and ambient effects, now sit in a **Miscellaneous** group, one sub-group each. None of them is on the default list. Footsteps, music and weather keep their own groups.
- **Player group:** it now holds only the player's own body sounds: hurt, death, jump, eating and drinking.
- **Combat:** new group for slap, stab, spear hit, projectile hit and throwing.
- **Crafting & Activities:** new group for everything else the player did that was listed under Player: panning, glue and cloth repair, knapping, clay forming, building, coins, collecting, chalk drawing, and so on.

## 0.1.2

**New**
- **Bell Mob slider:** one slider, "Bell Mob (Deep, Nightmare, Tainted)", for all of the Bell mob's sounds, including its deep bell. Deep, Nightmare and Tainted are the same mob and share these sounds. The Reception Bell stays on its own slider.
- **Charlemange slider:** "Charlemange (Mini Bell)" has its own slider, separate from the Bell mob.

**Changed**
- Charlemange's sounds are no longer on the default list. Search for them, or turn on "Show every sound".

## 0.1.1

**New**
- **NPC Voices slider:** one slider, "NPC Voices (Traders, Villagers)", for all NPC speech. Traders and villagers share overlapping voices, so they can't be told apart.

**Fixed**
- **Recently heard** now lists every sound that played in the last 30 seconds. Before, it also hid sounds that aren't on the default list.

**Known limit**
- NPCs from other mods that use their own voice files are not covered by the NPC Voices slider.
